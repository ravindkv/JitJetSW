The calorimeter trigger consists of two layers: Layer-1 
+ receives, 
+ calibrates, and 
+ sorts the local energy deposits ("trigger primitives") 
which are sent to the trigger by the ECAL and HCAL; Layer-2 uses these calibrated trigger primitives to reconstruct and calibrate the physics objects such as electrons, tau leptons, jets, and energy sums

The muon trigger system includes
+ muon track finders (MTF) which reconstruct muons in the barrel (BMTF), overlap (OMTF), and endcap (EMTF) regions of the detector, and the global muon trigger (µGMT, pronounced micro-GMT) for final muon selection. 
The µGT finally collects muons and calorimeter objects and executes every algorithm in the menu in parallel for the final trigger decision.

then the the Layer-2 calorimeter and  µGMT is combined  in the Global Trigger
