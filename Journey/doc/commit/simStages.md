# SIM stages: the Geant4 simulation switched on stage by stage, with SimAnalyzer plots

In the spirit of runCMSDrivers_mc2024_BBbar_GEN.sh (q qbar -> bbbar followed through ME, PS, MPI, Had, Decay,
GenJet), the detector simulation is now split into its physics ingredients, each an explicit cmsRun step 2
of the main script, and a SimAnalyzer draws what Geant4 did to the generator final state.

- Journey/test/runCMSDrivers_mc2024_BBbar_SIM.sh: SIM-only companion of runCMSDrivers_mc2024_BBbar.sh. Every stage
  is step 2 (process SIM) generated by cmsDriver.py (`step2 --filein <step 1 file> -s SIM --datatier GEN-SIM
  --eventcontent FEVTDEBUG --beamspot DBrealistic`, same conditions, era and geometry as the main script) on the step 1
  output of the full generator stage (GEN/step1_GEN_ME_PS_MPI_Had_Decay_out.root, generated with the GEN script if
  missing), with the Geant4 physics restricted through --customise_commands:
  Transport (no magnetic field, no decays, no EM, no hadronic physics: physics list JourneyTransportPhysics),
  Transport_B (+ UseMagneticField), Transport_B_Decay (+ G4DecayPhysics: FTFP_BERT_EMM with EMPhysics and HadPhysics
  off), Transport_B_Decay_EM (+ EMPhysics), Transport_B_Decay_EM_Had (+ HadPhysics = the standard SIM step).
  Without EM physics nothing deposits energy, so the stages 1-3 also set TrackerSD.ZeroEnergyLoss (the tracker
  records every sensor crossing as a PSimHit with eLoss = 0), G4TransportWithMSC = 0 and G4GammaGeneralProcess =
  False (no EM processes to couple to). Files per stage in SIM/: step2_SIM_<stage>_cfg.py (+ .driver.log),
  step2_SIM_<stage>_out.root (+ .log), simAnalyzer_<stage>_out.pdf, .root, .log. Variables: EVENTS (default 1, -1 =
  all events of the input), STAGES, INPUT, BEAMSPOT, OUTDIR, RUN=0, REUSE=1 (keep the stage outputs, rerun only the
  analyzers), ANALYZE=0, ANALYZE_EVENTS, GENSTAGES (GenPartAnalyzer pages before the SIM pages, default 5), DUMP*.
- Journey/plugins/JourneyTransportPhysics.cc: Geant4 physics list (plugin SimG4Core/Physics/JourneyTransportPhysics)
  that constructs all particles and registers no process, so that only the transportation that
  G4VModularPhysicsList adds itself is active. Needed for the two stages without decays: every standard CMS list,
  DummyPhysics included, registers G4DecayPhysics, and the UI commands of G4Commands are applied in the PreInit state
  before the processes exist, so '/process/inactivate Decay' has no effect.
  The release's simulation "biglib" (biglib/pluginSimulation.so) carries its own statically linked, hidden copy of
  Geant4, while this plugin links the shared libG4run.so; with both loaded, the PhysicsList base constructor ran in
  the biglib copy and RegisterPhysics in the shared one (null thread-local physics vector, segmentation fault). The
  script therefore runs the SIM cmsRun with the biglib directories removed from LD_LIBRARY_PATH, so that cmsRun takes
  the ordinary shared-library build of the simulation (same code and Geant4 version) for every stage.
- Journey/plugins/SimAnalyzer.cc, Journey/test/simAnalyzer_cfg.py: five eta-phi pages per event after the
  GenPartAnalyzer pages (genStages, default 5; the HLTBool filter trick of genJetAnalyzer_cfg.py keeps the order):
  1 primaries (status 1 genParticles with a SimTrack, gen link through the HepMC barcode, coloured by particle kind;
  the status 1 particles without SimTrack as grey crosses: |eta| > 5.5, p < 40 MeV, neutrinos are not tracked; the
  smeared primary vertex), 2 the primaries at the tracker / calorimeter boundary (SimTrack::trackerSurfacePosition /
  Momentum, filled by CaloTrkProcessing, with a line from the production direction showing the bend; primaries that
  never get there as open markers), 3 the secondaries at their production vertex coloured by the creator process
  (SimVertex::processType = Geant4 process sub-type: decay, ionisation, bremsstrahlung, pair production, Compton,
  photoelectric, annihilation, hadron elastic / inelastic, capture, at rest), 4 the tracker and muon PSimHits at
  their global position (GlobalTrackingGeometry) and 5 the PCaloHits summed per cell (CaloGeometry; HCAL ids
  relabelled from the test numbering with HcalHitRelabeller), marker size growing with the cell energy. Hit directions
  are taken from the primary vertex (the smeared vertex sits up to a few cm from the origin, which smears the eta of
  the pixel hits seen from the origin). The b / bbar systems are the stable decay products of the primary b hadron
  matched to each hard-process quark (last same-flavour copy, nearest primary hadron with the right b content, as
  GenPartAnalyzer stage 4); secondaries and hits belong to the system of their ancestor primary (SimVertex parent
  links, hit trackId / geantTrackId). Headers and stdout give the vector sums per system (pT at production and at the
  boundary, ECAL / HCAL / HF energies) and the net b + bbar; TFileService histograms per page (etaPhi, multiplicity,
  kind / process / subdetector distributions, b, bbar, net pT, ECAL and HCAL energies of the systems). The config
  loads GeometryRecoDB and the global tag (globalTag, era options) for the hit positions. Geometry lookups and the
  reco geometry are needed only for pages 4-5; the page layout, pdfFile / sampleLabel / openPdf / closePdf handling
  and the right panel (SIM stage of the sample highlighted with simStage) follow GenPartAnalyzer.
- Journey/plugins/BuildFile.xml: SimG4Core/Physics, geant4core, the geometry packages and DataFormats/DetId,
  GeometrySurface, GeometryVector.
- Journey/README.md: section on the SIM stages and SimAnalyzer.

- 63 of the 634 status 1 particles get no SimTrack: 56 with |eta| > 5.5, 6 with p < 40 MeV, 1 neutrino (the
  StackingAction kills neutrinos, TrackNeutrino = False). The b system (B- decay products) therefore has 8 of 9
  stable decay products in Geant4, pT 56.8 of 94.3 GeV: the missing 37 GeV is the neutrino of the semileptonic decay.
- Transport: no secondaries, no SimVertices beyond the generator vertices, the primaries keep their momenta and the
  tracker records the straight-line crossings (eLoss = 0); only HF has calorimeter hits, since its response is the
  parametrised shower library, not the Geant4 energy deposit. Transport_B: the same particles bend (page 2 lines),
  5 more low-pT primaries loop and never reach the calorimeter. Transport_B_Decay: 66 decay secondaries (the only
  process), one b-system primary decays before the boundary. Transport_B_Decay_EM: 678 EM secondaries (447 pair
  production / conversions, 228 bremsstrahlung, 20 delta rays), real energy loss (1.26 GeV in the tracker), ECAL and
  HCAL hits (EB 48.6, EE 188 GeV), hadrons punch through to the muon chambers (514 hits) since nothing stops them.
  Transport_B_Decay_EM_Had: 309 hadronic secondaries (305 inelastic), hadrons shower in the calorimeters (no muon
  hits in this event), E_ECAL(b system) = 73.6 GeV, E_HCAL visible 0.36 GeV.
- The ES subdetector code of DetId::Ecal is 3 (EcalPreshower), not 6: the 996 ES cells were first reported as "other".
- Hit directions seen from the origin smeared the pixel hits of a vertex at z = 3.1 cm over Delta eta ~ 1; taken from
  the primary vertex the tracks are lines of hits.
